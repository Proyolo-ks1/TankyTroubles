About Tank Trouble Remake

This is a 2-player local game remake of the classic Tank Trouble by Subterranean Software

In this game, you can control a tank and battle against other friend in an exciting arena. The goal is simple: destroy your opponent before they destroy you! The game features various mechanics, including power-ups and obstacles, that make each match unique.


I've taken the classic Tank Trouble and enhanced it with more game settings and features, while maintaining the same thrilling gameplay. By combining the best features from various versions and adding my own personal touch, this version brings together the best of both worlds for an even more exciting experience. Now, you can compete in intense battles and enjoy the retro feel of Tank Trouble right in your browser.
